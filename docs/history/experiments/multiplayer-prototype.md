# Retired multiplayer prototype

The ownership-based collaboration prototype was removed from the active examples
on November 5, 2026. The later product direction moved live collaboration to
[GangCode](https://github.com/chester-hill-solutions/gangcode). Stow supplies storage;
the prototype's coordinator, participant claims and dashboard are not Stow APIs.

The eight original files remain at
[`9d7c82bd43fc`](https://github.com/chester-hill-solutions/stow-s3/tree/9d7c82bd43fc62c9047081dac43ad0cc5f175570/examples/multiplayer-prototype).
That snapshot includes the runnable code, original commands, room semantics and
observations. Retrieve that revision when reproducing the historical experiment.
The `multiplayer`, `multiplayer-real` and `multiplayer-check` Make targets are retired.

Current examples are indexed in [examples/README.md](../../../examples/README.md).
