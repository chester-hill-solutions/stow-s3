# Buttermilk Pancakes

Serves 4 · 25 minutes

## Ingredients
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
- 1 cup buttermilk, plus more if needed
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Method
1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a second bowl, whisk the buttermilk, egg, melted butter and vanilla.
3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry and fold until just combined. A few lumps are fine. Rest the batter for 10 minutes.
4. Heat a lightly buttered skillet over medium heat. Scoop 1/4 cup of batter per pancake.
5. Flip when the edges look set and the bubbles hold, about 2 minutes, then cook for 1 minute more.
6. Serve warm with maple syrup.
