# Buttermilk Pancakes

Serves 4 · 20 minutes

## Ingredients
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons butter, melted

## Method
1. Whisk the dry ingredients in a bowl.
2. Beat the milk, egg and melted butter, then stir into the dry mix.
3. Cook on a hot griddle until bubbles appear, then flip and serve.
