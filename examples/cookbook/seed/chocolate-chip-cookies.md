# Chocolate Chip Cookies

Makes 24 · 45 minutes (including chilling)

## Ingredients
- 225 g unsalted butter, softened
- 200 g light brown soft sugar
- 100 g caster sugar
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 300 g plain flour
- 1 teaspoon bicarbonate of soda
- 3/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 250 g dark chocolate chips

## Method
1. Beat the softened butter with both sugars until pale and fluffy, about 3 minutes.
2. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well, then mix in the vanilla.
3. Fold in the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt until just combined, then stir through the chocolate chips.
4. Cover and chill the dough for at least 30 minutes. This stops the cookies spreading too thin.
5. Heat the oven to 180 C / 160 C fan. Scoop 40 g balls of dough onto lined trays, leaving room to spread.
6. Bake for 10-12 minutes, until the edges are set but the centres still look slightly underdone.
7. Leave on the tray for 10 minutes to firm up, then transfer to a rack. Sprinkle with flaky salt if you like.
