# Chocolate Chip Cookies

Makes 24 · 30 minutes

## Ingredients
- 225 g butter, softened
- 200 g brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 280 g plain flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 200 g chocolate chips

## Method
1. Cream the butter and sugar, then beat in the egg.
2. Mix in the flour and baking soda, then fold in the chocolate chips.
3. Drop spoonfuls onto a tray and bake at 180 C for 12 minutes.
