# Weeknight Tomato Pasta

Serves 2 · 25 minutes

## Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon chilli flakes
- 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- A handful of fresh basil, plus extra to finish
- 40 g Parmesan, finely grated, plus extra to serve
- Sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper

## Method
1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to the boil. Cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente according to the packet.
2. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil in a wide pan. Soften the onion with a pinch of salt for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and chilli and cook for 1 minute more.
3. Pour in the tomatoes and sugar. Season and simmer gently for 12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
4. Reserve a mugful of the starchy pasta water, then drain the spaghetti.
5. Add the pasta to the sauce with a splash of pasta water. Toss over a low heat until the sauce clings and looks glossy.
6. Off the heat, fold through the basil and Parmesan. Serve immediately with more Parmesan and basil.
