# Weeknight Tomato Pasta

Serves 2 · 20 minutes

## Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
- Salt and pepper
- Parmesan, to serve

## Method
1. Boil the pasta in salted water.
2. Fry the garlic in the oil, add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Drain the pasta, toss with the sauce and serve with parmesan.
