# Weeknight Tomato Pasta

Serves 2 · 25 minutes

## Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon chilli flakes
- 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- A handful of fresh basil
- Salt and black pepper
- Parmesan, to serve

## Method
1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water until just shy of al dente.
2. Soften the onion in the oil, then add the garlic and chilli and cook for a minute.
3. Add the tomatoes and sugar, season, and simmer for 12 minutes.
4. Reserve a mug of pasta water, then drain the pasta.
5. Toss the pasta in the sauce with a splash of pasta water until glossy. Tear in the basil and serve with parmesan.
